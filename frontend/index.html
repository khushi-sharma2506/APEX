From question bank to results in four steps

Teachers prepare once. Students get a calm, focused exam. Everyone sees only what they should.

1

Build the question bank

Drop in a CSV. APEX checks every row for subject, topic, unit, marks and options before anything is saved, and tells you exactly which rows to fix.

2

Set a blueprint, get a balanced paper

Choose topics, difficulty, marks, and how many questions to offer and attempt. APEX draws a paper without repeating a question and warns you if the bank runs short.

3

Students take a focused exam

One question at a time, with the next one already loaded. The difficulty adapts in the background, and nothing about it appears on screen.

4

Release results when you are ready

You decide how much students see: scores only, right or wrong, or a full review with correct answers. Results never release while the exam is still open.

One platform, two kinds of user

Teachers

  • Import questions from CSV with row-by-row checks
  • Set a blueprint and get a balanced paper
  • Choose how much students see afterwards
  • Mark subjective answers, then release results
  • Watch live question speed and cache performance

Students

  • See only the exams assigned to you
  • Get questions that match how you are doing
  • Have every answer saved as you go
  • Review your results when your teacher releases them

Fast when the whole class logs in at once

Waiting for the next question is the first thing students notice. APEX keeps popular questions in memory with a least-recently-used cache, and loads the likely next questions before the student answers.
